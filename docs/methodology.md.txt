# Methodology

## 1. Data Pipeline

Raw CSV files were loaded into Snowflake and organised into three layers:

RAW → STAGING → ANALYTICS

The RAW layer preserved the original source data.

The STAGING layer standardised fields, enriched records and performed data-quality checks.

The ANALYTICS layer contained business-ready tables used by Power BI.

---

## 2. Customer 360

Customer-level metrics were created at one row per household.

Key measures included:

- Total baskets
- Total shopping days
- Shopping days per 30 days
- Total sales value
- Average basket value
- Recency
- Average days between shopping days
- Campaign assignments
- Coupon redemptions

Average Basket Value:

Total Sales Value / Total Baskets

---

## 3. Customer Segmentation

Customers were scored using Recency, Frequency and Monetary value.

R = Recency  
F = Shopping Days per 30 Days  
M = Total Historical Sales

Customers were grouped into six behavioural segments:

- High-Value Active
- High-Value Inactive
- Frequent Lower-Value
- Occasional Higher-Value
- Recent Lower-Engagement
- Low-Engagement Inactive

---

## 4. Retention & Lapse

Two equal 90-day periods were compared.

Potential Lapse required:

- Shopping days decline >= 50%
- At least 3 shopping days lost
- Sales decline >= 25%
- Current recency >= 2x normal shopping gap

This identified 140 Potential Lapse households.

---

## 5. Campaign Analysis

Campaign response was calculated as:

Responding Household-Campaign Assignments / Total Household-Campaign Assignments

Campaign-period sales were compared with an equally long pre-campaign period.

These comparisons are descriptive rather than causal.

---

## 6. Market Basket Analysis

Category relationships were evaluated using:

Support — frequency of the pair appearing together

Confidence — probability of buying B when A is purchased

Lift — association strength relative to chance

Only pairs appearing in at least 500 baskets were retained.
